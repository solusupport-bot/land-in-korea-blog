# About Land in Korea

Land in Korea is an independent travel and lifestyle resource for first-time visitors and foreign residents in South Korea. We focus on practical, honest information—not generic travel listicles, but real comparisons, actual costs, and specific advice that helps you navigate Korean culture effectively.

## Our Mission

We believe that travel to Korea should be informed, confident, and enjoyable. Too many travel guides are either outdated, overly optimistic, or written by people who've never actually experienced the situations they're describing. We're different.

Every guide on this site is written from direct experience. We've taken the trains, missed the buses, eaten the street food, navigated the banking apps, and learned the cultural nuances firsthand. When we say something is safe, we've verified it. When we say something is expensive, we know the actual prices. When we recommend something, we've used it.

## About the Author

The author of Land in Korea has lived in Seoul for over a decade, worked with international companies, traveled throughout the country, and spent countless hours helping friends and visitors navigate everything from visa applications to hagwon selection to finding the best late-night restaurants.

This experience informs every post on this site. We're not here to sell tours or sponsored products. We're here to give you the truth.

## What We Cover

- **Practical Logistics** — Airport transfers, subway navigation, payment methods, currency exchange
- **Safety & Scams** — Real threats (very few) and targeted schemes (specific ones to avoid)
- **Cultural Etiquette** — What Koreans actually notice and why respect matters
- **Money Matters** — Real costs, saving strategies, tax refunds, card usage
- **Transportation** — KTX vs. SRT, luggage forwarding, regional travel comparison
- **Arrival & First Steps** — Everything from landing at Incheon to getting your first transit card

We intentionally avoid generic "100 Things to Do in Korea" lists. Instead, we write deep dives on specific situations you're actually facing.

## Privacy & Independence

This is an independent publication. We're not owned by tourism boards, travel companies, or hotel chains. Our recommendations are based on what actually works, not on sponsorship deals.

We do participate in affiliate programs (Klook, GetYourGuide, Booking.com, Trivago) for specific, bookable products (tours, transfers, accommodations). We only include affiliate links when there's a genuine, specific product tie-in, and we clearly mark all affiliate content. This helps support the site's operation, but it never influences our core advice.
