## What We Can Help With

- **Corrections** — Found an outdated price or error? Let us know.
- **New Topics** — Suggest a travel topic you'd like us to cover
- **Partnerships** — Press inquiries or legitimate partnership opportunities
- **General Questions** — We read every message

## What We Can't Do

- Provide real-time visa or legal advice (consult official sources)
- Book tours or accommodations for you
- Provide medical or emergency assistance

For emergencies in Korea:
- **Police:** 112
- **Fire/Ambulance:** 119
- **Tourist Hotline (English):** 1330
