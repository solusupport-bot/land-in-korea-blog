# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 2026-09-04

## 1. Introduction

Land in Korea ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") operates the landinkorea.com website (the "Site"). This privacy policy explains our data practices.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:
- Use our contact form
- Subscribe to our newsletter (if available)
- Post comments on articles
- Interact with our social media links

This may include your name, email address, and message content.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, we may automatically collect:
- **Device information** — IP address, browser type, operating system
- **Usage data** — Pages visited, time spent, referral sources
- **Cookies** — We use cookies for basic site functionality and analytics

We use GoatCounter for website analytics. GoatCounter respects privacy and does not use cookies for tracking. For details, see [GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/privacy).

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Improve our website and content
- Respond to your inquiries
- Send newsletters (if you've subscribed)
- Analyze site traffic and user behavior
- Prevent fraud and abuse

## 4. Affiliate Links

We may earn a commission through affiliate programs including:
- Klook
- GetYourGuide
- Booking.com
- Trivago

These links are clearly marked. Clicking an affiliate link does not change product prices for you. We only recommend products we've genuinely used or that align with our content.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Our Site uses services that may collect data:
- **Google Fonts** — Displays fonts from Google's server
- **YouTube Embeds** — If we embed videos, YouTube may collect viewing data
- **Social Media** — Links to social platforms
- **GoatCounter Analytics** — Privacy-respecting analytics

We don't control these third parties' privacy practices. Review their policies directly.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:
- Access data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Opt out of communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at [contact information].

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary:
- Contact form submissions are retained for one year
- Newsletter subscriber lists are retained until you unsubscribe
- Analytics data is retained per GoatCounter's default policy

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we've collected a child's data, contact us immediately.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We'll post changes on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the Site indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

## 10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or concerns, contact us through our [contact page](/contact).
