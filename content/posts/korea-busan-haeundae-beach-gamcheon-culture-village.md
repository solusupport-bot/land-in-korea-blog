## Two icons, opposite ends of the city

Haeundae Beach and Gamcheon Culture Village show up together in nearly every "one day in Busan" itinerary, but they sit on opposite sides of the city. Treat the trip between them as real transit time, not a quick hop — plan one for the morning and the other for the afternoon rather than trying to shuttle back and forth.

## Haeundae: the beach everyone knows

Haeundae is Busan's most famous beach, backed by a dense strip of hotels, cafes, and seafront restaurants. It draws its biggest crowds in summer, when the beach itself and the surrounding streets get genuinely packed.

## Gamcheon: stairs, not sand

Gamcheon Culture Village began as a hillside settlement in the 1920s-30s, later grew when refugees resettled there during the Korean War, and was transformed starting in 2007 when the city invited artists to turn the neighborhood into an open-air art village of murals, sculptures, and colorful houses. The result has earned it the nickname "the Machu Picchu of Korea" — but the neighborhood itself is almost entirely stairs and slopes, so comfortable shoes matter more here than the photos suggest.

## Getting there from Seoul

KTX from Seoul to Busan takes roughly 2.5-3 hours. In summer especially, it's worth booking ahead rather than assuming same-day seats will be available.
