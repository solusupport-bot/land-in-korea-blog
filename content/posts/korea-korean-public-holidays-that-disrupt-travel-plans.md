## The practical rundown

Chuseok (harvest festival) and Seollal (Lunar New Year) aren't just Korean holidays that closed a few shops — they're the two periods each year when a huge share of the country travels to hometowns simultaneously, jamming highways and selling out KTX and domestic flights days to weeks in advance. Landing a trip in the middle of either without checking the calendar first is one of the most avoidable planning mistakes for visitors.

Many small restaurants, markets, and family-run shops close for several consecutive days around Chuseok and Seollal, while large attractions, chain stores, and hotels generally stay open — so a itinerary built around market food stalls can fall apart during these windows.
