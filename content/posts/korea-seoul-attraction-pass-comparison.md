## What matters here

A Seoul attraction pass (bundling entry to multiple paid sites, sometimes with transit included) only pays off past a specific number of paid attractions per day — buy one for a light sightseeing day and you'll likely spend more than paying individually. Passes typically come in 24/48/72-hour versions activated from first use, not from purchase, so timing your first scan matters for getting full value.

The math that actually decides if a pass is worth it: add up the individual entry prices for everything you'd actually visit that day, then compare against the pass price — most people skip this step and just assume bundling is automatically cheaper.
