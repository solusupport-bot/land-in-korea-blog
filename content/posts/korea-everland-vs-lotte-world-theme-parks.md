## The short version

Everland and Lotte World both get called 'Korea's Disneyland,' but picking the wrong one for your trip wastes half a day on transit. Everland, in Yongin outside Seoul, is Korea's largest theme park with a zoo/safari section, and it requires a bus or shuttle ride from central Seoul — budget real travel time, not a quick subway hop. Lotte World, in Jamsil, is directly accessible by subway and combines an indoor park with an outdoor 'Magic Island' section, plus it sits next to Lotte World Tower and its observation deck.

If your schedule is tight, Lotte World's subway access usually wins over Everland's better rides — most first-timers don't realize the transit gap until they're already planning the day. Everland's outdoor layout and larger rides suit a full-day trip better, especially with kids who want the safari section. Lotte World works well as a half-day add-on to a Jamsil-area day since it's indoors and weather-proof, which matters more than people expect given Korea's summer heat and winter cold.
