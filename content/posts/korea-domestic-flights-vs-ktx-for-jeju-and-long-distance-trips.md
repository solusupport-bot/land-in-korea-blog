## What matters here

Jeju Island has zero rail connection to the mainland — no KTX, no regular ferry option fast enough for most trip lengths — so flying is effectively the only practical way there, and the Gimpo-Jeju route is one of the busiest domestic air corridors in the world with frequent departures. For mainland routes like Seoul-Busan, the calculation flips: KTX is often just as fast door-to-door once you factor in airport security, boarding buffers, and getting to/from airports versus city-center train stations.

Booking a domestic flight to Jeju without comparing total door-to-door time against KTX-plus-onward-transit for mainland trips is the most common miscalculation first-timers make. Domestic terminals at Gimpo and Gimhae (Busan) are separate from the international terminals, so don't assume international arrival/departure procedures apply — domestic flights use lighter security and no passport control.
