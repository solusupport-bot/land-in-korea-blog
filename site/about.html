About Land in Korea

Land in Korea is an independent travel guide based in Busan, South Korea. We publish practical information for visitors and foreign residents: transport, places to visit, payments and everyday life.

Our guides

Guides explain the choices travellers need to make and link to official sources for schedules, prices and procedures. Check the operator or government website before booking: details can change after publication.

AI tools assist with research and drafting. Land in Korea is responsible for the published material. These guides are reference articles; a photograph or description does not mean we personally tested every service.
