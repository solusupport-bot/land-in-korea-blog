Pick the situation you're actually facing — every guide below runs the real comparison, not another list of tips.

Most Korea travel advice tells you what exists. It rarely tells you which option is right for your trip length, group size, or budget — so you end up guessing at the airport counter anyway. These guides are written the other way round: each one takes a single decision a first-time visitor or new foreign resident actually has to make — airport transfer, data plan, transit card, tax refund, attraction pass — lays the options side by side, and says plainly which one wins in which situation, including when the answer is "none of them, skip it."

Prices, opening hours, and procedures in Korea change often. Every guide notes where a detail is time-sensitive and points you to the official source to confirm before you book.
